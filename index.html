I'm a Senior Product Designer and Design Lead who also writes code. At Texala Technologies I lead end-to-end product design for web and mobile, maintain our design system, and deliver WCAG 2.2 AA–compliant products. I use Claude Code to turn designs straight into working React prototypes, so stakeholders can try ideas sooner and engineers get a handoff they can actually build from.

Before Texala, I led product design at Sunnova Energy, first as Lead Product Designer on the consumer app and then as Manager, Digital Product Design. I hired and led a team of four designers, built the company's first user research program, and started the design system for the consumer app and dealer platform. Before that, I spent more than ten years building interactive and front-end experiences for brands like Disney, McDonald's, Hershey, Pepsi, and Ubisoft, and I taught UX/UI in an online bootcamp.

I'm based in Phoenix and open to remote senior and lead product design roles.
